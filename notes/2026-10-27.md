The repository is a home for small, independently useful artifacts: HTML experiments, plain-text notes, practical utilities, and short technical explorations.

## Working principles

- **Ship something usable.** Every file should solve a tiny problem, teach one idea, or record a concrete result.
- **Keep it lightweight.** Prefer plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and readable text before adding dependencies.
- **Explain the point.** Include a short description, usage instructions, or an example where it helps.
- **Avoid fake activity.** Do not make empty commits, duplicate files, or meaningless edits just to increase the count.
- **Check before publishing.** Never include secrets, credentials, private data, or copyrighted content copied at length.
